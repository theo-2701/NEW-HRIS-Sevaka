Overtime

Two stacked resources: the requests people file, and the daily summary that decides what is actually paid. Mode and category are derived by the server and cannot be typed. Payable hours are the smaller of hours actually worked and the total approved ceiling — never the number on the request.

The ceiling is the sum of every approved request on that date — one date may legitimately carry an advance request and a retroactive one. The reference column names only the first approved request as a trace of origin; it is never the source of the ceiling. Payable hours is the figure that flows to payroll — deliberately hours and category only, never money.
