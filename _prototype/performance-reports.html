Monitor & Reports

A read-and-assemble layer: no CRUD, no signal recomputation, no excusal decisions, no score writing. Everything here is derived from events owned elsewhere — the signal board by the recomputation sweep, the frozen history by sign-off, the access trail by whichever screen opened somebody's data. Changing a score still only ever happens through an objection. No score, no grade, no threshold column exists on this page.

Four kinds of empty, four different colours

"0 of 5" is a real finding, NO_RECORD is a live source that genuinely holds nothing, NOT_AVAILABLE is a source that did not answer, and NO_TASK_ASSIGNED means there was no denominator at all. Painting them alike would merge four different meanings. Group order carries no ranking of importance.

Read rights follow the chain as it stands today

Access is judged against today's reporting chain, not the chain that existed during the period — a former supervisor loses sight of history they once wrote themselves. That is a direct consequence of the read regime, not a defect. Every read of somebody else's history writes one access-trail row.

One door, one row

Writing the row is a precondition of the read, not a side effect of it: a refused read writes nothing, a successful read writes exactly one row per object per call. Reading your own board writes nothing at all — that is not access to somebody else's data.

HR only. Neither the subject nor the reader is given access to their own log, so the control function stays intact. This follows the standing pattern in the corpus rather than an explicit product decision, and is open to being overridden.
