Time Off Balance

A balance is never stored as a number someone types — it is the sum of every mutation in the ledger below. The grid is read-only in both directions; the single human-authored path into the ledger is an HR adjustment, and even that is create-only.

A year with no mutation has no row at all — that is not a zero balance.
