THR Checklist

We recommend you check a few things before run THR.

Make sure each employee's religion data is filled correctly — THR eligibility & schedule follow the religious holiday. View employee
Check employee length of service (masa kerja). THR is paid proportionally for those with less than 12 months. View employee
Ensure all THR-eligible employees are active and none have been resigned or transferred. View employee
Make sure the allowance components included in the THR calculation are up to date. Update component
Confirm the tax period and tax settings (TER method) are correct for this THR run. View settings
Verify the payment date complies with regulation — THR must be paid no later than 7 days before the holiday (H-7).
