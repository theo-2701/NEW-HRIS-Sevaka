Review Periods

A review period is the freezing anchor for every review sheet beneath it — scale_length and the assessment-structure pointer are copied into the row the moment the period is opened, and never re-read afterwards. Periods are never deleted; they only move through the three phases.

The grid carries a single row action. A period is never removed and never edited in place — it only transitions phase from its detail screen, and the button for a transition is rendered only when that transition is legal rather than shown disabled.

Eligibility Gaps

Two different concepts, so two separate tables — never merged. Both lists are computed live on every call and stored nowhere; nobody appearing here owns a review-sheet row for this period.

EmployeeReason

not_assessable[] — one reason only: NO_ASSESSOR_IN_STRUCTURE. The employee is not yet present in the assessment structure, so no assessor can be resolved.

EmployeeReason

out_of_assessment[] — two reasons: APPROVER_CHAIN_EXHAUSTED (top of the structure, final approver NULL or self) and NOT_INCLUDED_MID_PERIOD (joined after the period was opened and not added by HR).

An honest limit, not a bug

The APPROVER_CHAIN_EXHAUSTED computation does not check whether the company still has at least one active HR Manager — that lookup crosses schemas and was never decided as an automatic gate. An employee at the top of the structure whose final approver is filled in is legitimate and appears in neither table.

What the system freezes for you

The gate passes only when no other period of this company sits in FILLING. The system then reads the performance.* settings — assessment structure, period length, score scale — and copies the scale and the structure pointer onto the new row. A missing setting, a scale below 2, or a structure pointer no longer present in the group structure all reject the request.

Opening succeeds even when both eligibility lists are non-empty — they inform, they never block.

Start signing

POST /{id}/start-signing — an explicit HR trigger, never condition-driven automation.

No completeness gate

This transition does not check whether every sheet already carries an initial score. Moving to SIGNING also does not forbid the next period from sitting in FILLING — a signing tail is expected, not an error.

Close period

POST /{id}/close — CLOSED is terminal, there is no reopen path.

Terminal, and no approval gate

There is no "every sheet is signed" gate here. Unfinished sheets inherit the historical no-score state rather than being blocked at this step. On close, the sheet snapshots are formed and the period can never be reopened.
