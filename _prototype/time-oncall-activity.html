On Call Activity

A filtered read over the same overtime rows as the Overtime menu — only those born automatically from attendance falling inside an approved standby window. Terminal screen: nothing here leads anywhere else.

Read-only, with no manual add anywhere. A call-out that genuinely happened is recorded automatically from attendance — never typed by anyone. Approving or rejecting a call-out is not duplicated here either: that surface stays a single door in the Overtime menu.

The requested side is always blank: these rows are born automatically and never pass through a written request for hours. The approved side is copied from the window's ceiling and flows into the same overtime daily summary as a human request.
