## Deliberate deviations from the screenshots (kept, functionally equivalent)
- **C1/C2 are cards at the top of the Period tab**, not standalone pages, so the checker never lands on a screen whose only content is a diagram.
- **C7/C8 are sub-tabs of one detail card** under the grid (house standard: one table on screen at a time) instead of two separate pages.
- **D2/D3 and E4/E5 are one modal each** with two footer paths / a gate-dependent state, rather than four separate frames.
- **Reopen is offered from `LOCKED` only.** The state diagram also lists `REVIEWED → CALCULATED`, but `#28` requires the caller to be identical to `locked_by` *on that row*; a `REVIEWED` row that never carried a lock therefore has no eligible caller. The lifecycle card states this instead of offering a button that always answers `403`.
- **The screenshots' endpoint/error-code prose** stays condensed into modal and table notes rather than printed as page body copy.
