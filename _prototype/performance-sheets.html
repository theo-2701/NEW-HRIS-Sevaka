Review Sheets

One sheet is one employee × one period, walked through four system-enforced steps: the employee writes a self-assessment the supervisor cannot read; the supervisor fills the initial scores blind; the moment every line carries an initial score the self-assessment opens automatically — no button, no extra action; then the supervisor adjusts freely and submits.

What can and cannot be touched here

An initial score can never be overwritten once written — filled lines render locked, empty ones stay editable, and the refusal is the same code in the opposite direction. Master lines carry an HR-locked weight, so only their target may change and no delete button is rendered for them at all. Additional lines are free, but their share is measured against the 30% ceiling including the line being added. Adding or changing any line silently resets every read mark on the sheet and bumps the reset counter — the employee is asked to read the sheet again.

Frozen share is empty until submit

The frozen weight ratio stays NULL for the whole of IN_PROGRESS. It is written once, permanently, at submit time — which is also the moment raw weights stop being editable.
