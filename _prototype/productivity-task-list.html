Unmapped categories are absent on purpose. A category with no active mapping — Rapat & Koordinasi, for instance — is not rendered as an empty row. Hours logged under it freeze with a null paid-group snapshot: recorded, reported, never handed to payroll. Fail-closed by design.
Moving a category takes two steps. The foreign keys of an existing row can never be swapped — deactivate the old mapping, then create a new one. That is what keeps the historic paid-group snapshot on already-recorded hours meaningful.
