Benefit Reimbursement

Reimbursement claims for medical and other benefit expenses. The entitlement is reserved at submission (reservation_state=HELD) and consumed when the approval process completes. Medical attachments are access-restricted (§18.3).

Request No.EmployeeBenefit TypeAmountStatusReservationSubmitted

One grid, one endpoint (POST /benefit-claims/search). The row scope is forced by the server from the caller's role — a department manager sees their department and decides on SUBMITTED rows, a finance officer reads the whole company. The DISPUTE HOLD badge appears when an active hold targets the claim; a held row cannot be decided until the hold is released.

While a grace period is open two periods carry a live balance at once — pick the period to read one set of figures at a time; a single merged figure would mislead. Remaining is derived (entitled − used − reserved), never stored.

Remaining-after is derived client-side from row order — log_benefit_balance_ledger stores no balance column. entry_type carries ten values (GRANT, PRORATE_ADJUSTMENT, GRADE_ADJUSTMENT, EXIT_ADJUSTMENT, RESERVATION, RESERVATION_RELEASE, USAGE, USAGE_REVERSAL, FORFEITURE, NEGATIVE_FORFEITURE); the seeded scenario shows only a few. A USAGE row does not move the remaining figure — the reservation is converted, not deducted twice.

Family members you may claim for, valid per period (emp_benefit_beneficiary). Each row points at a relative already registered on your employee profile (mst_relative) — Finance keeps the pointer and the frozen relationship, never the family data itself, so a relative missing here must be added on the profile first. A row can only be deactivated — never hard-deleted — and a relationship outside the company whitelist is refused with 422 FIN_BENEFICIARY_NOT_LISTED.

Re-activating an inactive row: there is no employee-side path, and none was invented. PATCH /benefit-beneficiaries/{id} lets ROLE_EMPLOYEE† set is_active=false only (UIC §3.3); the same endpoint grants “koreksi” — including is_active=true, which log_benefit_beneficiary_history.changed_field records — to ROLE_FINANCE_OFFICER / ROLE_SUPER_ADMIN. Re-adding the relative is not an alternative either: uq_emp_benefit_beneficiary_employee_id_period_id_relative_id allows one row per relative per period, so a second POST collides. An inactive row therefore shows a disabled action for you — ask a finance officer to correct it, or wait for the next period, where a fresh row may be registered. Endpoints 2/4 carry no frame in the FSD screen map — recorded as a requested document change (proposed BR-A6), not improvised elsewhere.

mst_benefit_entitlement holds only benefit type × job grade × annual amount — the matrix is not tied to a period; a period plus this matrix produces emp_benefit_balance. Per-employee exceptions (mst_benefit_entitlement_exception) have no screen anywhere by design — read access is denied even to the employee the row belongs to.
