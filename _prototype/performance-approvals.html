Decide on sheet

POST /review-sheets/{id}/decisions — the answer is 202 FORWARDED, not 200.

The field does not exist for an approval — it appears, and becomes mandatory, only for a return or a final rejection.

What 202 means here

The response says the decision was forwarded to the workflow engine. The round outcome and the sheet status are written later, when the completion message comes back. The token carried forward to the engine is the approver's own token, not a service token.
