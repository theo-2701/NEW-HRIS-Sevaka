Notifications addressed to you only. Rows are created by the service from platform events — this screen reads them and marks them read; there is no create, edit or delete.

Scope notification:inbox:read / notification:inbox:write — every role, object-level (own data only). Rows belonging to another employee or another active company return 404 NOT_FOUND and never appear here.
