Reopen window

The due date has passed, or the form is closed, and one person still needs to answer — a new joiner, someone on sick leave. This reopens the form for that one person only.

Anonymous form — HR view

Answers with no respondent column, and deliberately no per-person tooling.

SubmittedItems
Zero buttons here, stated on purpose. No reopen window, no per-respondent filter, no edit. Filtering by respondent is refused with a 403 rather than an empty result — an empty result would itself leak whether a match was possible.
The audit column created_by is still written on an anonymous submission — that is system audit, not identity leakage; the domain respondent column stays empty. Invitations are still sent per person: the answer is anonymous, not the invitation.
