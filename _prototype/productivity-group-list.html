Group List

The master of work groups whose hours count as paid — the first filter in the hours-to-money chain. Work outside every group is still recorded and still reported; it is simply never handed to payroll.

No cascade — deliberately. Setting a group to is_active=false does not deactivate the Task List mappings that point at it. The two flags are independent: an old mapping keeps applying until HR switches it off itself.
There is no delete endpoint for this master. The row action stays visible and disabled with its reason rather than being quietly hidden — deactivation is the whole vocabulary here.
