PROB-FRONTEND-016
No employee-scoped read path for disbursement

“Reimbursement Taken” needs to read the disbursement marks for your own claims. The Disbursement module grants none of its twelve endpoints to ROLE_EMPLOYEE — the same root cause that keeps this list empty in self-service while the Finance module's Disbursement History tab is populated for a finance officer. Left empty deliberately: no endpoint was invented to fill it.
