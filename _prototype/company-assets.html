Asset status legend

Nine values of last_asset_status. INCOMPLETE ≠ NOT_AVAILABLE.

This legend is a build / requirements reference — it exists so every one of the nine status values can be verified during development. In the shipped product it can be hidden or condensed; it is not a primary end-user surface.
INCOMPLETE is informational and non-blocking — the asset is still assigned. NOT_AVAILABLE is a hard blocker (missing branch/category) that keeps the asset out of the pool.
