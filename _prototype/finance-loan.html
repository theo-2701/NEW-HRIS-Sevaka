Who can change what. A finance officer cannot alter principal or tenor, and cannot cancel or withdraw on an employee's behalf — cancel, withdraw and early settlement stay with the employee who owns the loan. Installment start date and arrears handling follow the schedule and are visible there, not edited on this screen.

Acknowledge repayment schedule

Principal, interest and tenor as received from the funding party. One endpoint, two outcomes: ACK approves the loan, DECLINE releases the reservation.

Sent from inside the application after a full login — the request is never accepted from a notification link.
Decline is a contracted outcome, not a cancellation. POST .../schedule-acknowledgements carries outcome: ACK | DECLINE; DECLINE writes loan_status = DECLINED_BY_EMPLOYEE, releases the reservation and lets you submit a new request. Cancel is a different action on a different status — it only exists while the request is still SUBMITTED — and Withdraw applies once AWAITING_CALCULATION passes its threshold. Calling either outcome outside AWAITING_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT is refused with 422 FIN_LOAN_NOT_AWAITING_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT.
