Cash Advance

Two submission doors (by the employee, or created by a finance officer on their behalf), then a two-stage settlement: the finance officer flags receipts, the recipient's manager decides. Any difference is computed automatically at the closing stage.

The recipient is the identity that matters — for a request created on someone's behalf, the row belongs to the recipient, not to the creator. Repudiation is available to the recipient only, only on a request created on their behalf and not yet disbursed. Travel cancellation needs no approval gate, and turns the whole amount into a surplus at the closing stage.

Stage 1 — the finance officer flags receipts (review, returns 200 with UNDER_REVIEW): a marking, not a decision. Stage 2 — the recipient's manager decides (decision, returns 202): the final status and any difference are written asynchronously.
